1. Executive Summary
Project Overview

InterviewIQ AI is a next-generation AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help students, fresh graduates, job seekers, and professionals prepare for technical, HR, behavioral, coding, aptitude, and system design interviews through personalized, AI-driven learning experiences.

Unlike existing interview platforms that offer static question banks or basic mock interviews, InterviewIQ AI delivers dynamic interviews that adapt to each user's responses, analyzes resumes against job descriptions, provides recruiter-style feedback, tracks progress over time, and generates personalized preparation roadmaps.

The platform combines Large Language Models (LLMs), speech processing, online code execution, analytics, and modern web technologies to create a realistic interview environment that mirrors real hiring processes.

The project is intended to serve three goals:

A polished college major project.
A resume-quality portfolio project.
A scalable SaaS foundation that can evolve into a production product.
2. Vision Statement

Our vision is to build an AI interview coach that feels like having a personal recruiter available 24/7.

The platform should:

Prepare candidates for interviews at startups, service companies, and product companies.
Adapt interviews dynamically based on performance.
Continuously learn from a user's history.
Help users improve rather than simply evaluate them.
Deliver recruiter-quality reports after every session.
3. Problem Statement

Candidates often face several challenges:

They don't know what questions companies ask.
They lack realistic interview practice.
They receive no meaningful feedback.
Resume optimization is difficult.
Existing ATS scanners are limited.
Coding platforms don't simulate interviews.
HR interviews are difficult to practice.
There is no single platform combining resume analysis, AI interviews, coding assessments, and learning recommendations.

InterviewIQ AI addresses these issues through one integrated platform.
